About Campus-Eats

At Campus-Eats, we believe that an effective delivery system to the comfort of students and staff members is our top priority. A quick and reliable once-in-a-lifetime service has come to the rescue where anything will be delivered safely with 0% defects and dissatisfaction.

Founded in April 2025 by a passionate group of Studentpreneurs, we are in our introductory phase across all South African universities. To date, we have assisted 100+ of our target market within different institutions of higher learning.

Our Core Values:

  • Reliability: Consistent service you can count on
  • Timely Delivery: Fast service that respects your schedule
  • Affordability: Student-friendly prices
  • Quality Service: Excellence in every delivery

Though we haven't even been in business for 2 months, we have already exceeded our initial target numbers predicted at the start of the business.
